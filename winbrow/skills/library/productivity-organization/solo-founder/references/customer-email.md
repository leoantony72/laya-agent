# Customer Email

## Goal

Write a clear, human email that resolves the customer's issue or moves them to a
decision.

## Structure

1. **Subject** — specific, no clickbait.
2. **Context** — acknowledge the exact issue in one sentence.
3. **Answer** — what happened, what you did, or what you need.
4. **Next step** — one action, with a date or a link.
5. **Close** — short, no corporate filler.

## Tone

- Own the mistake without over-apologizing.
- Do not blame the customer or a third party.
- No "we value your feedback" unless you state the action taken.
- Use the customer's name and the product terms they used.

## Rules

- Never promise a refund, credit, or timeline you cannot authorize.
- Do not include internal IDs unless they help the customer.
- Keep it under 150 words unless the issue needs detail.
- For a sensitive issue, offer a call instead of a long thread.

## Output

Subject line + email body + one-line internal note on what to do next.
