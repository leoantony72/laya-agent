# Client Data Explainer

Use this template when a client asks what happens to their data in an AI tool.

## Template

```
# How we handle your data in <tool>

## What we put in
<Documents, prompts, and metadata that may be processed.>

## Where it is processed
<Local machine, approved cloud provider, or region. Name the provider.>

## Retention
<How long prompts and outputs are kept, and who can access them.>

## Training
<Whether the provider trains on the data. State the contract term.>

## Human review
<Who can review the material and under what policy.>

## Your controls
<Deletion, export, opt-out, and contact route.>

## Limits
<What the tool does not do; where a human must verify.>

Last reviewed: <date>. This is an operational description, not legal advice.
```

## Rules

- Never claim "we do not store anything" unless the provider contract proves it.
- Name subprocessors and regions.
- Keep it under one page.
