# Firm AI Policy (Skeleton)

Adapt this skeleton to the firm's jurisdiction and risk posture. This is a
template, not legal advice.

## Sections

1. **Purpose and scope** — who and which tools are covered.
2. **Approved tools** — named tools, versions, and owners.
3. **Prohibited uses** — client identifiers in unapproved tools, autonomous
   filing, generating citations without verification.
4. **Data classification** — public, internal, confidential, privileged.
5. **Human review** — every AI output affecting a client must be reviewed by a
   qualified person.
6. **Verification** — citations, calculations, and factual claims must be
   checked against primary sources.
7. **Confidentiality** — provider terms, retention, training, subprocessors.
8. **Security** — access control, logging, incident reporting.
9. **Client communication** — when and how AI use is disclosed.
10. **Training** — onboarding and annual refresh.
11. **Exceptions and review** — approval route and review cadence.
12. **Enforcement** — consequences and audit.

## Minimum Controls

- A named owner for each tool.
- A register of approved tools and data flows.
- No client data in a tool without a signed data-processing term.
- A documented verification step for citations and quoted language.
- An incident path for accidental disclosure.
