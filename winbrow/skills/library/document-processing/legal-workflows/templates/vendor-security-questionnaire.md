# Vendor Security Questionnaire (Short Form)

Send this to an AI or SaaS vendor before sending confidential material.

## Company and service

1. Legal entity, registered address, and service description.
2. Where is data hosted (country/region)? List subprocessors.
3. Which certifications apply (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001)? Provide the report
   date and scope.

## Data handling

4. What data is stored, for how long, and how is deletion requested?
5. Is customer content used to train models? Quote the contract term.
6. How is data encrypted in transit and at rest?
7. Who can access customer content, and is access logged?
8. How are backups protected and deleted?

## Security

9. SSO/MFA support, role model, and admin audit log.
10. Vulnerability management and penetration-test cadence.
11. Incident notification timeline and contact.
12. Business continuity and disaster recovery targets.

## AI-specific

13. Which model providers are used, and can they change without notice?
14. Are prompts/outputs retained by the model provider?
15. Is there a human review or abuse-monitoring process?
16. Can the customer opt out of retention or training?

## Contract

17. Data-processing agreement, breach liability, audit rights, termination.
18. Governing law and data-export terms.
